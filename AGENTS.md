# Project Rules & Preferences (Persistent Memory)

## Critical Design & UI Constraints

1. **NO UNNECESSARY HINTS OR EXTRA LABELS:**
   - Never add helper hints, secondary labels, fallback options (such as "Default Mail", "Or launch email directly", "Copied to clipboard" tooltips, extra instructional subtitles, etc.) in any page or component across the entire project.
   - Keep interfaces completely clean, uncluttered, minimal, and premium.

2. **CLEAN & DIRECT USER ACTIONS:**
   - Interactive elements must perform their core intent directly and cleanly without intermediate modals, forms, or secondary cluttered buttons unless explicitly requested.
   - For email/contact: clicking the direct contact action should immediately launch the email compose window without redundant hint text or alternative buttons.

3. **PRESERVE EXACT DESIGN STRUCTURES:**
   - Adhere strictly to the requested aesthetic and component layout without injecting extra widgets, forms, or auxiliary elements.
